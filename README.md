This is rock simulator! A game I made a year ago, in 2025, and is honestly actually fun!
Basically the whole premise is you are a rock; and you wait for things to happen. Every
second counts as currency, and you use the currency to buy new skins. There are also
weather events that can happen that give special skins. Seems boring, doesn’t it?
Well, you may be right... for now... because over the years, I’ve made 13 updates.
And each update brought more things, up until where we are now, the mining update. Now,
there is a whole minigame you can play, and a minigame in that minigame! There’s so
much gameplay now, and all you have to do is keep waiting to find out what’s next.
